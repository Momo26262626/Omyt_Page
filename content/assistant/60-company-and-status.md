# Company, status and getting involved

## Where omyt is right now

- **Invite-only**, building with a small number of design partners.
- Each brain is currently **built by hand** against the partner's own systems.
- The **hosted product (SaaS) opens in Q4 2026**.
- The first two functions omyt runs are **sales and marketing**. The design is not
  limited to those, but that is the deliberate starting scope.

## Who it is for

Businesses running real sales and marketing at enough volume that a model has
something to reason about — where the cost of forgetting (a deal in a thread
nobody reread, a campaign that ignored what sales already learned) is easy to
measure. It does not work on a blank slate.

## omyt runs on omyt

omyt is its own first customer. This company's own go-to-market — sourcing,
research and outreach — is run by the brain. The team builds the product by using
it on themselves.

## Contact and next steps

- To get involved, use the application form on the site (the waitlist / "Apply"
  section). Everyone gets a reply, including an honest read on fit.
- For anything else: hello@omyt.ai.
- Legal and company details are on the Imprint page; the Privacy page covers how
  the site handles data.

## What this assistant can help with

Questions about what omyt does, how it works, how it differs from other tools, the
design-partner programme, and how to get involved. For technical diligence beyond
the published material — including the internals of the withheld architecture
layers — the right path is a direct conversation with the team, which the form
will start.
