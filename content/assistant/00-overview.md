# omyt — overview

omyt is a company brain. It holds everything a business knows — every account,
campaign, thread and outcome — reasons across all of it continuously, and acts on
what it concludes. Today that means sales and marketing run themselves.

It is not a CRM, not an analytics dashboard, not a chat copilot bolted onto
another tool, and not an AI SDR that blindly sends sequences. The difference is
that omyt holds one persistent model of the business and connects its conclusions
directly to execution.

## What it does

- **Sources** the accounts worth knowing, enriches them, and scores them against
  what has actually converted for this business.
- **Understands** each one from every signal it gives off, and keeps that
  understanding current rather than resetting between sessions.
- **Acts** — outreach, follow-ups, reply routing, list-building — inside
  guardrails the customer controls.
- **Learns** from every outcome, so the model sharpens over time.

## The three words

- **Persistent** — nothing resets between sessions. Every conversation, deal and
  result stays in the model and keeps informing the next decision.
- **Semantic** — it holds meaning, not just fields. It knows a pricing visit, a
  quiet champion and a sector tailwind are related, and what that implies.
- **World model** — one connected picture of the whole business that omyt can
  reason across and run forward.

## Status

Invite-only. Each brain is currently built by hand with a small number of design
partners, against their own systems. The hosted product opens in Q4 2026.

## Guardrails

- One emergency stop halts every outbound channel at once.
- Per-day send caps, suppression lists and a bounce-rate circuit breaker.
- Draft-only mode: the brain writes and queues, a human releases.
- Strategy can be frozen; every suggestion can be approved or vetoed, and both
  train what comes next.
