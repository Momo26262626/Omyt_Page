# How omyt is different from the tools you already have

Visitors often ask how omyt compares to a CRM, an AI SDR, a dashboard, or a
copilot bolted onto another tool. The short version: those tools each hold a
slice of the truth and forget the rest. omyt holds one persistent model of the
whole business and connects its conclusions directly to action.

## Versus a CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio)

A CRM is a filing cabinet. It stores rows and waits for a person to open it,
read it, and decide what to do. It does not reason, it does not act on its own,
and the picture only exists when someone reassembles it. omyt reads a CRM as one
of its inputs, but it holds meaning rather than fields, keeps that understanding
current, and moves on it. A CRM is where you record what happened; omyt is what
decides what to do next.

## Versus an AI SDR (11x, Artisan, and similar)

An AI SDR sends sequences. It is a faster way to do outbound, but it fires
whether or not the timing, the account, or the message is right, and it does not
learn across the rest of the business. omyt sources and writes outreach too, but
only as the execution end of a model that already knows why this account, why
now, and what happened last time. The outreach is a consequence of the reasoning,
not the product itself.

## Versus a dashboard or BI tool

A dashboard recites the numbers. It tells you what is true and leaves the
interpretation and the action to you. omyt does the interpretation: it surfaces
the single highest-value move with the reasoning attached, rather than a wall of
charts you have to read.

## Versus a chat copilot inside another app

A copilot answers questions about the tool it lives in. It has no memory of the
last conversation and no reach beyond its host app. omyt is not a copilot on top
of a system of record; it is the system that holds the whole picture and runs.

## The one-line difference

Every other tool holds part of the business and waits. omyt holds all of it,
reasons across it continuously, and acts. That is what "company brain" means, and
it is why the value compounds instead of resetting.
