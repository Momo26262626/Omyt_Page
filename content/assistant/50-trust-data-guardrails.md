# Trust, data and guardrails

A system that acts on its own has to be governed, not improvised. omyt is built so
the customer holds the controls and the brain compounds within them.

## Guardrails on action

- **One emergency stop** halts every outbound channel at once.
- **Per-day send caps** and warm-up pacing protect the sending domain.
- **Suppression lists** (unsubscribes and bounces) are enforced automatically.
- **A bounce-rate circuit breaker** pauses sending if deliverability drops.
- **Draft-only mode**: the brain writes and queues, a human releases. The
  customer decides how much autonomy it gets, and can dial it up as trust builds.
- **Every suggestion is approved or vetoed**, and both train what comes next.
  Strategy can be frozen.

## Data

- During the design-partner program, omyt runs against the customer's own
  systems. Their data does not leave their infrastructure.
- Nothing about one customer's business trains anything shared across customers.
- The value comes from persistence — the model holds the customer's own history
  and reasons over it — not from pooling data between companies.
- A hosted option arrives with the SaaS in Q4 2026.

## Reasoning you can read

Every decision omyt surfaces comes with its rationale. If you cannot see how it
reached a conclusion, it is not finished. It proposes with the "why" attached;
the customer decides.

## What the assistant will not do

This website assistant answers from published material only. It will not describe
the internal implementation of the withheld architecture layers, will not invent
customers, metrics or prices, and will route anything it does not know to a
conversation with the team via the form on the site.
