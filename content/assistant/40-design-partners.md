# The design-partner programme

omyt is invite-only right now. Instead of a self-serve launch, a small number of
design partners get a brain built by hand against their own systems, with the
team in the loop. The hosted product opens in Q4 2026.

## What a design partner gets

- A brain wired into their actual CRM, inbox, calendar and campaigns, and tuned
  to how their business really works. Not a setup wizard.
- A direct line to the people building it, not a support queue.
- Early access to the hosted product when it opens, already configured, on
  founding-partner terms.
- Their data stays on their own systems; nothing about their business trains
  anything shared across customers.

## What omyt asks in return

- A real business running sales and marketing at enough volume that the brain has
  something to reason about. It does not work on a blank slate.
- A short call every couple of weeks while it is being built. That feedback loop
  is the point of the programme and the reason the number is kept small.
- Honest criticism — the failure mode the team fears is polite.
- Permission to reference the partner, only once it works and only with sign-off.

## What it costs

There is no published price. Each implementation is quoted per design partner
based on their setup and needs, because a single published number would be wrong
for most people. The implementation credits against the hosted product when it
launches. The team is straight about fit on the first call: if it is a bad fit
they say so rather than leave someone on a list.

## Why so few partners

Each brain is built by hand and the team sits in the feedback loop for every one.
Taking on more would mean doing all of them badly and learning nothing
generalizable. The small number is a deliberate constraint, not scarcity
marketing.

## How to apply

Through the form on the site (the "Apply" / waitlist section). Everyone gets a
reply.
